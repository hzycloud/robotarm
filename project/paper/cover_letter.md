Dear Editor,

We are pleased to submit our manuscript entitled "Edge-Sort: Closed-Loop Desktop Sorting via Tiny Distilled ACT Policies on a Low-Cost Edge Robotic Arm" for consideration in IEEE Sensors Journal.

This work addresses the gap between demonstration-level TinyML robotic systems and deployable edge autonomy. Compared with prior work that reported 7.6 s recognition and 14.6 s open-loop grasping on a UR5, we present a closed-loop sorting system on a low-cost SO-ARM101 arm with a Raspberry Pi 5. The system includes a distilled and int8-quantized TinyACT policy for real-time edge inference, a grasp verifier with automatic retry, an energy and latency benchmark, and an open-source dataset in LeRobot format.

The topic fits the journal scope on embedded sensors, machine learning, and robotic systems. All experiments were conducted on physical hardware, and data and code will be released upon acceptance.
